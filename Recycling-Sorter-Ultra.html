        How this works
        1. Camera. The browser's getUserMedia gives us a live video stream, only after you press Allow.
        2. AI model. MobileNetV2, a neural network pre-trained on ImageNet (1,000 object classes), runs inside the browser with TensorFlow.js. The fast scan looks at the current frame and returns its top 10 guesses. A separate on-demand Deep Scan uses a zero-shot image/text model to compare one photo with an expanded catalogue of more than 100 item descriptions.
        3. Waste mapping. The expanded keyword dictionary maps common object labels to ten suggested waste streams: plastic, metal, paper, organic, glass, e-waste, wood, hazardous/special items, textiles, and residual/mixed waste. The optional Deep Scan compares the photo with specific descriptions such as plastic toys, cans, batteries, electronics, wood and common packaging.
        4. Hand and face guard. MobileNet has no "hand" or "face" class, so shown a hand it names the nearest object, such as a paintbrush. Before trusting it, MediaPipe's hand and face finders measure how much of the ring a hand or face covers, with a skin-colour check as backup. If one fills the ring and the item guess is weak, the app says "Hand" and refuses to sort. A confident guess still wins, so a bottle held in your hand works. The AI also sees only the square around the ring, so a face in the background is ignored.
        5. Smoothing. A category has to win a vote over the last 5 readings, so one bad frame cannot fool the sorter.
        6. Hold and sort. The same category must stay detected for 2 seconds while the ring fills. Then points are awarded, the streak grows, the bin lights up and the field notes teach the facts.

        Honest limits: these models recognise visual patterns, not material chemistry. A visual match cannot reliably verify polymer types (PET, HDPE, PVC, PP, PS or ABS), metal alloys, brand or exact product SKU. Deep Scan is an expanded candidate comparison, not a database of millions of products, and its scores are relative rather than calibrated probabilities. Always verify material markings and local recycling guidance. Decomposition figures are broad estimates; actual conditions vary. The hand and face finders download from the internet; without them the app falls back to the skin-colour check. Good light and a plain background give the best results. The optional Deep Scan downloads a larger model from the internet only when you first use it. It can take time and bandwidth; the fast camera scanner remains available if Deep Scan cannot load.
